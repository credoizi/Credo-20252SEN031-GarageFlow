# GarageFlow – Vehicle Repair Management System

GarageFlow is a Vehicle Repair Management System designed to help a garage organize customer, vehicle, repair, invoice and payment information.

## Phase I – Business Process Modeling

### Selected Business Process
**Vehicle Repair Management Process**

This process describes how a customer brings a vehicle to the garage, how the receptionist records the customer, vehicle and repair request, how the mechanic inspects and repairs the vehicle when necessary, and how the cashier records the invoice and payment before the vehicle is returned.

## Phase I Information Activities

- **Create:** Customer, vehicle, repair request, invoice and payment records.
- **Retrieve:** Customer, vehicle and previous repair information.
- **Update:** Inspection findings, repair progress, repair status and payment status.
- **Archive:** Completed repair and payment records for future reference.
